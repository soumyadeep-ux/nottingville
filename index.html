Common Questions

Everything parents ask

Is Nottingville a hostel or a PG?
Both, really. Nottingville works as a girls hostel in Durgapur and as a PG (paying guest) accommodation. The difference from a typical PG in Durgapur is that we provide full supervision — wardens, CCTV, curfew, and four meals a day. Think of it as a managed student hostel with the comfort of a paying guest setup. Many students and parents search for a girls PG in Durgapur — that's exactly what we are, with more structure and care.
What is the room rent at Nottingville?
Room rent in Durgapur varies by hostel — at Nottingville, rent starts at ₹6,000 per month for non-AC rooms. AC rooms cost more depending on the configuration. This includes 4 meals daily, 24/7 security, housekeeping, and all utilities. Two months' rent is payable at admission, and monthly rent is due by the 10th of each month.
Is this a girls-only hostel?
Our main hostel locations are a dedicated girls hostel — also described as a ladies hostel or women's PG. We also have a separate apartment for boys. Both are fully supervised with the same safety standards. WhatsApp us to learn more about either option.
Do you provide meals or is there a mess facility?
We provide all four meals in-house — breakfast, lunch, evening snacks with tea, and dinner. It's a full mess facility included in your rent. The food is home-cooked daily with a rotating menu that includes non-veg six days a week. No outside food or personal cooking is allowed.
Is it safe for students living away from home?
Safety is why we exist. Nottingville is a safe hostel with 24/7 CCTV across all common areas, resident wardens, a strict 7 PM curfew, daily attendance registers, and night escort by wardens. The hostel is run by three mothers — the same standards they'd want for their own daughters.
Is there a dormitory option available?
Yes. We have shared dormitory-style rooms as well as smaller rooms for students who prefer more privacy. All options include the same facilities — meals, security, and supervision. Contact us on WhatsApp for current availability and pricing for each room type.
How close is Nottingville to coaching centres?
Very close. Our locations on Sarojini Naidu Path are within walking distance of Adhyayan Durgapur, Physics Wallah, Aakash Institute, and Allen Career Institute. Students preparing for NEET UG, JEE Main, JEE Advanced, BITSAT, and CUET save hours of daily commute.
How do I book a bed?
WhatsApp us at +91 79089 78959 or call any of our numbers. We'll arrange a visit so you can see the hostel yourself. Admission requires two months' rent upfront and a 30-day notice period before vacating. Seats fill quickly each session — we recommend enquiring early.
